# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Participants should communicate constructively, assume good faith, respect
different viewpoints, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than
people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit comments, close contributions, or ban
participants for behavior that violates this standard. Report concerns through
the private channel described in `SECURITY.md` or by opening an issue that asks
for maintainer contact without including sensitive details.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
